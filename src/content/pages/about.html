Our Story

A New Era for Authentic Thai Traditions

From the vibrant shores of Phuket, Chef Lee brings twenty years of professional mastery to the kitchen of our beloved and local Thai restaurant today.

Growing up surrounded by Southern Thai seafood culture gave Lee an early connection to fresh ingredients and the bold, vibrant flavours of his island homeland.

Now under new ownership, we are proud to continue the wonderful local legacy that Mui built here with so much care over the years.

Lee’s journey across the UK hospitality industry is driven by a passion to share genuine Thai soul with a community that appreciates truly authentic cooking.

We believe that great food brings people together. Our kitchen uses only the finest herbs and spices to ensure every plate reflects our true heritage.

Honouring the foundations established by Mui is our priority. We maintain the same warm hospitality and welcoming spirit that has always made our guests smile.

From our family to yours, we invite you to experience the genuine warmth and unforgettable tastes that make Aroy Thai such a very special place.
